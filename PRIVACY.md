# Privacy Policy — YouTube Stat Eliminator

**Effective:** October 2026

YouTube Stat Eliminator does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal
data. Period.

- **What the extension stores:** only your four on/off toggle preferences
  (view counts, like counts, subscriber counts, comment counts), saved with
  `chrome.storage.sync` so your choices follow your Chrome profile. No
  browsing history, no watch data, no identifiers.
- **What the extension reads:** the text of YouTube pages you already have
  open, solely to find and hide engagement statistics. This page content never
  leaves your browser.
- **Network:** the extension makes no network requests of its own. All code
  runs locally; there is no remote code, no analytics, no tracking pixels.
- **Permissions:** the extension requests only the `storage` permission
  (to remember your toggles). It does not request tabs, history, cookies,
  or access to any site other than YouTube pages where it hides stats.
